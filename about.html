about_me.txt

I've been designing for the past five years, and I've never gotten bored of it. I'm always hungry to learn more. Design is ever-evolving, and that's what I love about it. For me, it's a portal to show my empathy, self-expression and taste. Other than that, I love thinking creatively and ideating. I have wild ideas, mostly about the short films I want to make, the articles I want to write, the paintings I want to paint, the eyeshadow looks I want to experiment with, and all things creative.

I have a really good taste :) (Men I may not know, but design I know.) My friends always ask me before buying an outfit, or anything for that matter, because trust me, I know ball. Would love to yap more about this over a meet! I love talking too, so see you there.

— Anushka
